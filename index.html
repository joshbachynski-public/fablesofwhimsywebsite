Welcome, fellow reader

We are a collective of readers who believe a book is best enjoyed out loud, over coffee, and in wonderful company — so we build the evenings to prove it.

What we conjure

An almanac of gatherings

Brunch & Bookstore Crawls

Coffee, pastries, and a guided wander through the loveliest independent bookshops in town.

Whimsical Workshops

Bookbinding, botanical journaling, and small crafts made by candlelight with tea in hand.

Candlelit Book Club

An evening of cordials and conversation as we turn this season's read over beneath the stars.

Enchanted Events

Masquerades, storytelling salons, and themed soirées with a hidden door or two in the stacks.

The feeling we're after
