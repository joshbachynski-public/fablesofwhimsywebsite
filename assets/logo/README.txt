YOUR LOGO GOES HERE
===================

There's a placeholder wordmark in this folder (fables-logo.svg) so the
site looks finished right now. To use your real logo:

  • Replace  fables-logo.svg  with your "fables 2 logo.svg"
    (keep the filename EXACTLY  fables-logo.svg  and it swaps in everywhere —
     favicon + browser tab included).

  • If your logo is a PNG instead of SVG, name it  fables-logo.png  and do a
    find-and-replace of  fables-logo.svg  →  fables-logo.png  across the
    .html files (5 files).

The small emblem drawn inside the nav bar and footer is inline SVG (part of
the page markup). If you'd like it replaced with your real emblem too, just
say so in your next session and it's a quick swap.
