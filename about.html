Our story

A collective for the
incurably bookish

We began with a simple, stubborn idea: that the best part of reading is the moment you get to share it.

How it started

Two cups, one open book,
and a table of strangers.

Fables of Whimsy grew out of small mornings — a handful of readers meeting over coffee to talk about the books that had kept them up too late. Word spread. The table got longer. Someone suggested we wander to a bookshop afterward, and something clicked.

Today we curate gatherings that feel less like events and more like stepping into a favourite story: brunch & bookstore crawls, candlelit workshops, a book club that meets when the sky turns violet, and the occasional masquerade among the shelves.

What we believe

Stories are meant to be lived in — not just finished. We build rooms where that can happen: warm, a little magic, and open to anyone who loves a good tale.

What to expect

Every detail, a small enchantment.

  • ✦  Gatherings small enough to actually meet people
  • ✦  Independent bookshops & local makers, always
  • ✦  Candlelight, cordials, and coffee done properly
  • ✦  A keepsake to carry the evening home with you
  • ✦  A warm welcome whether you came alone or with ten friends
